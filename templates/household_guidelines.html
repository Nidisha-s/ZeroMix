Household Waste Segregation Rules

  • Separate wet and dry waste every day
  • Use green bin for food and kitchen waste
  • Use blue bin for plastic, paper, metal, glass
  • Do not mix plastic with wet waste
  • Clean containers before dry disposal
  • Keep batteries and medicines separate
  • Give only segregated waste to collector
  • Show your household QR code during pickup
  • Proper segregation earns reward points
  • Repeated mixing may lead to fines

Do ✅

  • Use two bins at home
  • Segregate waste daily
  • Keep dry waste clean and dry
  • Follow bin color codes

Don’t ❌

  • Mix food and plastic waste
  • Pour liquids into dry waste bin
  • Hide mixed waste
  • Skip QR code during collection
